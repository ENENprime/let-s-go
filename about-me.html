How everything began

I was born and raised in Cameroon, a country located in Central Africa. I have always been fascinated by the world of technology and the endless possibilities it offers. This curiosity led me to explore various programming languages and web development frameworks, which has become a significant part of my life.


My journey into the world of technology started in primary school. At that time we were placed in groups of 6 in order to type our names on a word document. So seeing how it was really difficult to manage it like that, I had to ask a computer from my Dad which gave after some times of reflexion. Now I could follow up the class, practice once while exploring the different facinating softwares at home. Years passed and I entered the world of secondary school, where my aim then was to remain among the best like I always did. My elder sister who did studies in topographing frequendly asked me to type her school graduation projects in exchange to some AUTO CAD tips. My passion for coding started in Form 4 with HTML and C introduced to us. I was amazed by how these languages could bring ideas to life on the web and how the computer could execute C instructions. Over time, I beginned to develop some deep feelings for the subject.
